Behind the stitches

Slow craft in a fast world

Hi there, I'm Clara, the creator and hands behind Love and Loop Crochet. What started as quiet evening therapy has grown into an artisanal studio dedicated to reviving heirloom textile crafts.

Every piece is created by hand using sustainable botanical dyes, organic non-mulesed wool, and GOTS-certified unbleached cotton.

“We believe objects in your home should hold warmth, memory, and the gentle touch of human hands.”

From an amigurumi friend to a chunky wool throw, each creation is made to bring quiet comfort through years of cozy memories.

Clara LindqvistFounder & fiber artisan
