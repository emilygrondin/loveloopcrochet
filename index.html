Behind the stitches

Slow craft in a fast world

Hi there, I'm Eva, the creator and hands behind Love and Loop Crochet. What started as a leisurely activity has grown into an artisanal studio dedicated to creating crocheted items for everyone to love.

Every piece is created by hand with soft yarn in a wide variety of colors.

"I believe objects in your home should hold warmth, memory, and the gentle touch of human hands.”

From a cute, cuddly friend to an elegant bouquet of flowers, each creation is made to bring quiet comfort through years of cozy memories.

Eva GrondinFounder & fiber artisan
